## Could INVARIANT actually do better?

The [current architecture](/Users/nikchin/Desktop/invariant/docs/ENVIRONMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md) supplies persistent isolated Linux execution. Its filesystem checkpoint does not capture external SaaS state or provide application-aware database recovery. (P-C015)

**Technical inference:** preserving a local environment does not reproduce Make's hosted engine, Airtable records or a provider outage. A local checkpoint cannot undo an external CRM write. Nor does it define whether a missing business record is correct. To compete on this workflow we would need integrations, representative test state, business assertions and controlled changes to those external systems. That is substantial new product work; a persistent shell alone does not provide the advantage. (P-C016)

The narrowest hypothesis worth evaluating is **handover and repair of inherited Make/Airtable operations when the original specialist leaves**. It has a clearer owner and budget signal than our earlier generic ideas. User: replacement automation specialist; likely payer: founder/operations lead. Those buyer roles are hypotheses informed by the posts. This is **a research lead, not the selected direction**: there are not yet three unrelated named commercial teams reporting the same costly residual failure after trying credible alternatives, and no comparative proof of superiority. Do not implement an MVP or attach a price on this basis. (P-C016)

## A test that could change the decision

Before a build recommendation, obtain authorized, redacted records from three unrelated teams: the failing workflow, prior repair attempts, actual time spent, the relevant native recovery features used, and the business outcome required. Compare the same incidents using the native tool plus a specialist, a coding agent with equivalent access, and an INVARIANT prototype. Measure hands-on repair time, correct records, duplicates, regressions and onboarding effort. Require a material recurring improvement without shifting setup work to the customer. Stop if native configuration resolves the cases, external-provider behavior dominates, or the coding agent alone performs equivalently. These are proposed criteria, not completed experiments. No outreach is authorized or performed. (P-C016)

## Limitations and evidence records

English-language, purposive, non-exhaustive public desk research through October 18, 2026. Searches covered data integrations, business automations, analytics and WordPress maintenance; security and generic agent infrastructure were excluded as product directions. Hiring budgets are intentions, not purchases; incident duration is not hands-on labor. Forum author affiliation, current fixed releases and commercial identity remain unknown where stated. Positive vendor stories only enter as checks against a proposed gap. There is **moderate evidence of ongoing operational work**, **low evidence of a specifically underserved niche**, and **no demonstrated INVARIANT performance advantage**. (P-C001, P-C016)
