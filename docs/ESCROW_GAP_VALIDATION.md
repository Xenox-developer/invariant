# Escrow build-verification gap: rejected

**Decision, October 18, 2026:** reject independent build-verification workbenches as INVARIANT's next niche. This bounded public search did not find a convincing set of verification firms reporting recurring, costly rebuild work inadequately served by Escode, Codekeeper and VM workflows. **The earlier pilot recommendation is withdrawn.** Paid verification work establishes a service category, not unmet demand for our software. This is a decision on available evidence, not proof that no private opportunity exists. (X-C001)

## What would have qualified

We sought attributable commercial practitioner accounts describing recent engagements, repeated hands-on rebuild cost, their actual tools, the remaining failure after using them, and a task INVARIANT could improve. A customer buying escrow, a service fee, a generic warning about incomplete deposits, or an automation pitch does not meet that test. These are analyst inclusion criteria. Current residual labor cost, frequency, purchasing intent and switching motivation remain unknown. (X-C001)

## What INVARIANT would change

Our [architecture](/Users/nikchin/Desktop/invariant/docs/ENVIRONMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md) documents persistent Linux filesystems, without running-process preservation or environment export/import. An escrow evidence workflow is not implemented. This is documentation review, not a runtime assessment. (X-C012)

**Analyst inference:** persistence could retain tools and intermediate state. It cannot provide absent vendor source, disclose a password, obtain redistribution rights or make suppliers answer sooner. A saved repaired environment does not establish that a new verifier can rebuild from declared materials; separate fresh verification remains necessary. Our incremental labor advantage is unmeasured. (X-C013)

## Action and reconsideration

Do not build escrow-specific manifests, evidence exports or a verification dashboard from this evidence. Withdraw the prior comparative-pilot recommendation. No outreach, trials, purchases or product implementation occurred. (X-C001)

Reopen only with attributable recent evidence from multiple independent commercial verification firms: repeated labor or cost, their VM/agent/service baseline, the precise remaining task, why products or a simple script are insufficient, and credible buying motivation. A later comparison would need the same coding agent in the firm's existing environment. These are proposed gates, not discovered demand. (X-C015)

## Method and limits

Public English-language purposive desk research through October 18, 2026. Searches combined software escrow, verification consultant, rebuild, manual work, costs, delays, snapshots and VMware; checked providers, practitioner testimony, customer cases and official VM documentation. General software testing, security research and financial escrow testimonials did not qualify as rebuild-demand evidence. No representative sample or prevalence estimate. Missing publication dates remain `not-stated`; a crawl date or updated retrospective is not a recent incident. Product pages establish advertised scope; private tooling remains unknown. (X-C001)
